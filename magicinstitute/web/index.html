The story is the wrapper.
The skill is the point.

Every quest teaches one real procedure, step by step, and the platform guarantees it. Each step becomes exactly one gesture, in order. The story can't skip, reorder or invent a step, and the build is rejected if it tries.

Example: using a fire extinguisher, in the tower of Sleeping Beauty.

  1. Size up the fire☝️ point
  2. Raise the alarm🤏 pinch
  3. Grab the extinguisher✊ grab
  4. Pull the pin🤏 pinch
  5. Aim at the base☝️ point
  6. Squeeze the handle✊ grab
  7. Sweep side to side👋 wave
  8. Watch, then back out👍 thumbs-up
